# Research Note: Λ as the Geometric Face of Coarse-Graining

**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** conjecture, notebook grade — below C+ in the ledger vocabulary; not paper-ready
**Series:** loose conjectures, dated at capture
**Arose from:** the refinement-graded graininess prediction (foundations paper, P3) combined with the closure-saturation reading of horizons (Part IV), prompted by the observation that long chains in emergent spacetime are long accumulations of refinement.

## The conjecture

Cosmic expansion, in emergent spacetime, is the geometric representation of
progressive coarse-graining along observer chains: as records accumulate, the
unresolved multiplicity available to any chain shrinks, and the cosmological
constant is the geometric residue of that gradient. The cosmological event
horizon is the saturation surface of the observing chain — the locus at which
accessible closure is exhausted and no further correlation with what lies
beyond can be encoded. On this reading Λ is not an energy density but a
capacity statement: the geometric face of how much refinement remains.

## A correction baked in at capture

The intuitive phrasing "records store less and less" has the direction
backwards, and the flip matters. Along refinement, records *accumulate*;
what shrinks is the *unresolved* multiplicity — what remains recordable.
Saturation is capacity filling, not draining. The "edge of the visible
universe" is therefore not where information degrades but where the chain's
accessible closure runs out: nothing left that this chain *can* record about
what is beyond. Same endpoint as the naive phrasing; opposite mechanism.

## Prior art (the idea has company)

- **Banks (2001)** — already cited in the foundations paper as motivation for
  finiteness — proposes the static version outright: the cosmological
  constant is not derived but *is* the specification of the number of degrees
  of freedom, dim(H) ~ e^(S_dS) ~ e^(10^122). Λ finite ⟺ state space finite.
  The framework cites this in one direction (Λ motivates finiteness); this
  note conjectures it runs the other way too (finiteness geometrizes as Λ).
- **Sorkin's "everpresent Λ"** (Ahmed, Dodelson, Greene, Sorkin, PRD 69,
  103523 (2004), with roots in Sorkin's 1990s work) — in causal set theory,
  the framework's named nearest neighbor, discreteness of the spacetime
  element count yields Λ fluctuating at order 1/√N, and predicted the
  observed order of magnitude before the 1998 supernova results. The closest
  existing realization of "Λ as a counting residue."
- **Verlinde (SciPost Phys. 2, 016 (2017))** — dark energy from the
  information-theoretic side of emergent gravity; neighboring territory.

## Guardrail (recorded so the wrong version is not developed)

The photon-side version — graininess accumulating per interaction along a
photon's flight path, "each particle of dust a device" — is closed by
observation. Detected light from distant sources is the light that did *not*
interact (absorption is extinction: dimming, not degradation); the CMB is
pristine since last scattering; and interaction-accumulation theories of
redshift (tired light) fail the image-sharpness, supernova time-dilation, and
Tolman surface-brightness tests. The framework's own stability criterion
explains the physics: interactions leaving no stable record do not refine, so
photon mileage is not refinement. Any development must be built on the
*chain-side* version — the P3 gradient over position in the refinement
ordering of the observing chain — not on photon odometry.

## What a real version owes (in order)

1. **The continuum limit** (frontier problem: g_ab from the discrete
   refinement lattice). No geometry, no geometric face of anything.
2. **Saturation ⇒ de Sitter.** "Capacity runs out" must be shown to induce
   constant-curvature asymptotics specifically — de Sitter is a particular
   geometry, and the derivation must produce that shape and not another.
3. **Expansion history.** Λ dominates only late; the conjecture must recover
   the FRW sequence, not just the asymptote.

## Open quantitative questions

- Scaling: is the geometric residue of order 1/M_s (rounding character) or
  1/√M_s (fluctuation character, Sorkin-like)? Plausibly tied to the
  integer-apportionment open problem (CHSH paper ledger).
- If Λ is the gradient's geometric face, it is not constant in refinement
  order — an evolving dark-energy equation of state would be the natural
  expectation, which current surveys probe. In-principle contact only, at
  this grade.
- Relation between the saturation surface of *one* chain and the
  observer-independence of the measured Λ: why do all chains see the same
  value? (Presumably the redundancy/objectivity machinery, but unargued.)
