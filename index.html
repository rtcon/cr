Configuration Realism

A foundational theory of physical reality grounded in timeless configuration space, finite records, and emergent dynamics.

1. Postulates

The theory rests on five foundational postulates.

Postulate 1 — Timeless Configuration Space
  • Physical reality consists of a timeless configuration space \(\Omega\).
  • Each configuration \(\omega \in \Omega\) specifies a complete arrangement of fundamental degrees of freedom.
  • Configurations do not evolve in time.
  • Configuration space carries complex phase structure; the phase associated with a configuration contributes to quantum amplitudes.
Postulate 2 — Finite Physical Records
  • Physical information exists only in the form of records.
  • A record \(r\) is a finite physical arrangement of degrees of freedom encoding correlations among components of configuration space.
  • Because records are finite physical systems, record encoding is necessarily lossy.
Postulate 3 — Compatibility Classes

Each record defines a compatibility class

\[ \Omega_r = \{\, \omega \in \Omega \mid \omega \text{ is compatible with the correlations encoded in } r \,\}. \]
  • Compatibility classes represent the set of configurations indistinguishable with respect to the correlations encoded by the record.
  • Compatibility classes are observer-relative and generally overlap.
Postulate 4 — Closure Geometry of Records
  • Records encode correlations that logically imply additional correlations.
  • These implications are captured by closure operators defined over record structures.
  • Closure structure regulates the refinement relations among records and induces an invariant distinguishability measure called closure distance \[ d_c(r_1, r_2) \] representing the minimal restructuring required for one record to encode the correlations of another.
  • Closure distance accumulates discretely \[ d_c = N\,\delta_c \] where \(\delta_c\) is the closure quantum, the minimal distinguishable increment of correlation-encoding capacity.
  • Record structures therefore form a refinement lattice, with closure distance corresponding to minimal refinement chains within this lattice.
Postulate 5 — Emergent Dynamics
  • Observers are record-bearing subsystems capable of encoding ordered correlations among record states.
  • Observers encounter ordered refinement chains of records within the refinement lattice.
  • Apparent physical change arises from traversal of these refinement chains.
  • This emergent description of ordered refinement is called closure dynamics.
  • Time, motion, and spacetime trajectories therefore correspond to coordinate representations of closure structure rather than fundamental evolution of configurations.
